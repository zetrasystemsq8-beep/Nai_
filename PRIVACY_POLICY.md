# Privacy Policy for NAI — Nigeria's AI Assistant

**Last updated:** July 2026

NAI ("the app") is developed by Zetra Systems. This policy explains what information NAI collects, how it's used, and your choices.

## Information We Collect

**Account Information**
When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password. Authentication is handled securely through Firebase Authentication (Google). We do not store your password directly — Firebase manages this securely.

**Conversations**
Your chat conversations with NAI are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload or store your conversation history on our servers.

**Usage Data**
When you ask NAI a question, your message may be sent to:
- Groq (AI language processing), to generate a response
- NewsAPI and Wikipedia, to retrieve relevant real-world information

These services process your query text to generate an answer but are not used to build an advertising profile of you.

## How We Use Information

- To provide and improve NAI's chat and information features
- To authenticate your account and keep it secure
- We do not sell your data to third parties
- We do not display third-party advertisements in NAI

## Data Storage and Deletion

- Chat history is stored locally on your device and can be permanently deleted at any time via Settings > Clear Chat History
- Deleting the app also removes all locally stored conversation data
- To delete your account entirely, contact us using the details below

## Third-Party Services

NAI uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:
- **Firebase (Google)** — authentication
- **Groq** — AI response generation
- **NewsAPI** — news content
- **Wikipedia** — reference content

## Children's Privacy

NAI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as NAI evolves. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
